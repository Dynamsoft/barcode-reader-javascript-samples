📱 Scan with your phone — keep this page on your desktop

Barcode scanning works best on a phone camera. Send any sample to your phone in seconds, then use the barcode images on this screen as scan targets.

  1. Point your phone camera at a QR code — the one here opens this page, and each sample has its own.
  2. Try the sample on your phone.
  3. Scan the barcode images shown on this screen.
